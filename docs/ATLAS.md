# Project Atlas

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Planning Phase

---

# Vision

Project Atlas is the official portfolio of Deepak Polisetti.

It is designed to present Deepak as an AI & Data Analytics professional through thoughtful storytelling, exceptional engineering, and refined user experience.

The portfolio is not intended to be a simple personal website. It is a long-term professional product that will evolve throughout Deepak's career.

---

# Mission

Build a portfolio that earns trust through craftsmanship, clarity, and real work rather than visual excess or exaggerated claims.

Every section, animation, interaction, and design decision must contribute to credibility, usability, and professionalism.

---

# Primary Goal

Create a portfolio that helps recruiters, hiring managers, and technical interviewers quickly understand:

- Who Deepak is.
- What he has built.
- How he solves problems.
- Why he should be interviewed.

---

# Brand Position

AI & Data Analytics Professional

The portfolio should communicate practical engineering ability, analytical thinking, curiosity, and continuous learning.
